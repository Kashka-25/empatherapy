You don't need to reach breaking point to deserve support.

Finding someone to talk to can feel like a big step, especially when you're already carrying a lot. HOPEIUM is a simple way to reach out, see what support is available, and arrange a conversation without having to navigate a complicated process.

Part of something bigger.

Empatherapy is the wider home for practitioners, holistic approaches and ongoing support. HOPEIUM is its quick-connect pathway: a gentle first step towards human connection, with room to explore further support when you're ready.

Simple by design

One small step at a time.

No pressure to know exactly what you need before you arrive. We can begin with a conversation.

01 / Reach out

Tell me a little about what would help.

You can share as much or as little as feels comfortable. A polished explanation isn't needed.

02 / Find a time

Arrange a conversation.

During the beta, Cassidy's available times are confirmed personally. You'll know the format, length and contribution before anything is booked.

03 / Find your next step

Leave with something to carry forward.

Where useful, the conversation can include a simple grounding practice, reflection or one follow-up check-in.

A human being on the other side

Meet Cassidy.

HOPEIUM begins with Cassidy, the founder of Empatherapy. His approach is grounded in compassionate listening, holistic counselling and complementary practices, with each conversation shaped around the person and what feels appropriate for them.

The intention isn't to fix you or tell you who to be. It's to offer a respectful space where you can feel heard, explore what is present, and reconnect with your own capacity to choose a next step.

“Every part of you is trying to show up for you. You deserve the space to understand what it's asking for.”

Support that meets your circumstances

Care should feel accessible.

The HOPEIUM beta is exploring a sliding contribution model. The amount you choose is a conversation starter, not a judgement about what you can afford. We'll confirm the contribution with you before a session is arranged.

The first stage is intentionally small and founder-led. As the model develops, HOPEIUM may welcome other Empatherapy practitioners and their availability.

The first chapter · Founder-led beta

A gentle beginning.

HOPEIUM is starting small, with Cassidy offering a limited number of conversations while the experience is tested and refined. Availability is not 24/7, and every request is personally confirmed. This helps build a thoughtful service before inviting a wider practitioner network.

HOPEIUM offers non-emergency emotional support and is not a crisis response service or a substitute for emergency, medical or mental-health care. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Please don't include sensitive personal details in an initial email.
